Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: non-playable overview concept art for the surface-world strategic map of a seasonal multiplayer medieval survival RPG.
Input images: CA-007-mid-season-hex-territories-v1.png is the composition target: preserve its wide near-top-down orthographic map language, regular flat-top hexagonal territorial grid, continuous river and roads, and two separated kingdom clusters. CA-001-first-household-v4-grounded-low-poly.png is the authoritative low-poly style reference: match its clean faceted geometry, simple angular silhouettes, restrained adult tone, muted natural colors, broad readable forms and low visual noise.
Primary request: create a cleaner, more atmospheric far-zoom surface map with almost no local detail. Demonstrate territorial control, three knowledge states, continuous geography, broad development density and a few discovered neutral landmarks.
Composition: wide 16:9, near-top-down orthographic view with a slight three-quarter tilt. One finite inland region made of roughly 30–36 large equal flat-top hexagonal segments. Western rust-red kingdom controls a substantial connected cluster on the left; eastern slate-blue kingdom controls a substantial connected cluster on the right. They do not touch anywhere. A broad belt of neutral segments separates them.
Three knowledge states aligned exactly to whole hex segments:
1. Terra incognita: several complete hexes around parts of the outer perimeter and a few frontier gaps are covered by deep opaque charcoal-black fog. Absolutely no terrain, ownership color, road, structure or landmark is visible beneath it. The viewer cannot tell whether a black hex is neutral or controlled.
2. Previously explored / last-known: most known hexes are covered by a restrained translucent cool dark veil. Terrain, roads, broad development and last-known ownership remain readable, but clearly look older and less immediate.
3. Currently observed: a small connected group of known hexes remains at normal daylight brightness and full color, demonstrating current information.
Territory treatment: regular clean hex outlines remain visible only on known segments. Use subtle rust-red and slate-blue surface tint plus restrained border accents for last-known control. Neutral known hexes keep olive-green and earth-brown terrain. Black terra-incognita hexes reveal no border color and no side.
Far-zoom content only: show one continuous low-poly river, broad forest masses, open plains, low mountains and a few major roads that continue naturally across hex boundaries. Do not depict individual houses. Represent settlement and industry only as sparse abstract low-poly clusters or broad patches of development density: a small compact roof mass, a field patch, a quarry scar, a short wall contour. A tiny simplified town-hall silhouette may anchor controlled segments but must not dominate or repeat as a large detailed building.
Neutral points of interest: in two already explored neutral segments, show rare visually distinct but still simple surface landmarks. One is a modest ancient stone entrance or ruin suggesting a neutral dungeon below ground. Another is a natural neutral source of rare resources, such as an unusual pale mineral outcrop or crater-like deposit. They have no faction color, no giant icon, no label, and no magical spectacle. Terra incognita never reveals hidden landmarks.
Visual hierarchy: first the black terra incognita shapes; second the two separated kingdom masses and known neutral belt; third the continuous river, roads and terrain; fourth the sparse development density and two neutral landmarks. Must read clearly at thumbnail size.
Lighting/mood: soft overcast daylight on current segments; stale segments slightly darker and desaturated; terra incognita almost black with a restrained soft smoky low-poly surface. Clean, sober, exploratory mood.
Constraints: preserve the physical-world feeling under a strategic map overlay. Low-poly and clean, not painterly, not photorealistic. Fog boundaries follow entire hexes, never a noisy freehand texture. Keep the map sparse.
Avoid: individual house-by-house detail, repeated large town halls, armies, characters, UI panels, labels, text, numbers, legend, compass, icons, glowing markers, giant fantasy dungeon portals, giant crystals, third faction, kingdom contact, raised board-game tiles, gaps between hexes, literal walls around every hex, parchment style, realistic satellite imagery, painterly texture, clutter, watermark.
